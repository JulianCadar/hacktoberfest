CORE MECHANICS & RULES:
1. Party & Vitals:
   - 1 to 4 players.
   - Each player begins with exactly 5 hearts (max_hearts: 5).
   - If hearts <= 0, the player is dead (hearts = 0, is_alive = false) and permanently eliminated.
   - The game ends (game_over: true) ONLY when all players are dead (is_alive = false for everyone).

2. Inventory Constraints:
   - Each player has exactly two equipment slots: 1 "weapon" and 1 "item" (utility, consumable, or relic).
   - If a player acquires a new weapon/item when that slot is full, the previous one is discarded or replaced.
   - Existing items in inventory should actively influence narrative solutions and provide tactical advantages when relevant.

3. Turn Progression:
   - Players take individual turns in a round-robin sequence: Player 0 -> Player 1 -> ... -> Round completes -> Round increments.
   - SKIP eliminated players entirely during turn cycling.
   - When the last surviving player in the roster finishes their turn, increment round_number by 1 and loop back to the first living player.

4. Action Consequences & Team Dynamics:
   - Every player choice and round has direct cause-and-effect impact on the narrative trajectory. several rounds may follow a single event.
   - Actions can harm, protect, or heal the acting player, an ally, or the entire party.

   - Every 2 to 3 rounds, present opportunities to scavenge, discover, or craft tools, weapons, or items.

5. Output Expectations:
   - For every turn, resolve the previous player's action with concrete consequences: explicit narrative outcome, health changes, and inventory modifications.
   - Advance the plot to the next living player's situation.
   - Provide exactly 4 diverse, mutually exclusive choices (A, B, C, D) tailored to the acting player's specific equipment, role, and the current threat.
